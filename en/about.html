Our Story

Infologix was founded in 2026 with a clear vision: transforming the logistics sector through technological innovation and smart solutions.

We started as a small team of experts specializing in the food delivery sector, and today we have become a strategic partner to the largest logistics companies in the region. We deeply understand the challenges of this sector and provide solutions specifically designed to address them.

Our journey hasn't been easy, but our commitment to quality and innovation has helped us build a strong reputation as a reliable company that delivers real solutions that make a difference in our clients' operations.

Key Achievements
  • Serving over 50 companies in the logistics sector
  • Developing Faris platform - the leading delivery management solution
  • Reducing operational costs for clients by up to 35%
  • Increasing operational efficiency by up to 45%
